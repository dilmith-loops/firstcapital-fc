# First Capital Investor Week Landing Page

## What I’ll build
- Replace the blank home page with a mobile-first First Capital landing experience using the supplied navy, gold, and white branding.
- Add the supplied First Capital logo in the header and a clear Investor Week label.
- Build the opening section around the approved “You Already Know How to Start.” message, the four investor personality visuals, the full quiz disclaimer, and the highlighted “Find My Investor Type” action.
- Add the investment-options, “Why First Capital?”, and final account-opening sections using the client’s exact supplied wording and destination link.
- Include the supplied contact details and disclaimer in a focused footer.
- Give each highlighted action strong gold treatment and useful destinations, with smooth in-page navigation where applicable.

## Content boundaries
- The attached brief says the seven quiz questions and personality reveal copy are in a separate quiz document, and that testimonials will be shared later. I will not invent that missing client-approved content.
- The landing page will therefore provide the quiz entry point and the complete supplied educational content, while keeping the unavailable quiz/results/testimonials material out until provided.

## Visual direction
- Predominantly white canvas with deep navy typography, disciplined gold accents, compact borders, and restrained motion.
- Four cohesive illustrated personality avatars as the primary visual feature, designed to feel approachable for first-time investors.
- Clean, fast, highly legible layouts across phone and desktop sizes.

## Technical details
- Store the supplied logo through the project asset service and import its asset pointer.
- Generate and import four lightweight avatar illustrations.
- Use semantic design tokens for all brand colors and controls.
- Add complete page metadata and verify the live page at desktop and mobile widths.
